# 1.6.2

## 中文

新增「硬件加速」开关（设置 · 外观）：默认开启。如果你的机器上整个应用都卡顿、拖动窗口不流畅（通常是 GPU 进程异常或显卡驱动兼容问题导致渲染回退），可以关闭它改用软件渲染——对文字界面来说软件渲染同样流畅，而且更稳定。更改在重启应用后生效。

同时启动时会在日志中输出 GPU 合成状态，便于判断整体卡顿是否来自渲染回退。

## English

Adds a "Hardware acceleration" switch (Settings · Appearance), on by default. If the whole app feels laggy and window dragging stutters on your machine — typically a GPU process or driver compatibility issue — turn it off to use software rendering, which is smooth and stable for a text-first UI. The change applies after restarting the app.

The app also logs the GPU compositing status at startup to help diagnose whether lag comes from a render fallback.

Windows installers are unsigned. Mac installers are ad-hoc signed, with no Developer ID signature or Apple notarization.
