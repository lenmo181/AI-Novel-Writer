# 1.7.0

## 中文

作品资料新增两个工具页。「全书检索」在正文最新稿、章节蓝图、角色卡与架构设定里查关键词：多个词用空格隔开、都要出现才算命中，结果分组列出原文摘录与命中数，点一条直接跳到对应页面。「写作统计」把作品的进度与用量放到一页：已动笔章数、全部与定稿字数、每章字数条形图、最近 30 天的 AI 调用与产出字数活动、按模型与用途的调用量统计——数字全部来自本地记录，只读展示。

导出升级：定稿章节可以打包成 EPUB 3 电子书（带目录），并新增「只导出部分章节」的范围选择，对合并 Markdown、分章 Markdown、TXT 与 EPUB 全部生效。

修复角色名单生成对「先输出草稿 JSON、再输出修正版」的推理模型的解析；硬件加速开关保存失败时的报错改为按界面语言的说明。

## English

Two new tool pages under Reference. "Book search" looks up keywords across the latest manuscript text, chapter blueprints, character cards and story settings: separate multiple words with spaces and every word must appear; results are grouped with text excerpts and match counts, and clicking one jumps straight to the page. "Writing stats" puts a book's progress and usage on one page: chapters written, total and finalized word counts, per-chapter word bars, the last 30 days of AI calls and generated words, and per-model/per-purpose usage — every number comes from local records, read-only.

Export upgrades: finalized chapters can be packaged as an EPUB 3 e-book (with a table of contents), and a new "export only part of the book" chapter range applies to merged Markdown, per-chapter Markdown, TXT and EPUB alike.

Also fixes character-roster parsing for reasoning models that emit a draft JSON first and a revised one after, and the hardware-acceleration switch now reports save failures in the interface language instead of a raw error code.

Windows installers are unsigned. Mac installers are ad-hoc signed, with no Developer ID signature or Apple notarization.
